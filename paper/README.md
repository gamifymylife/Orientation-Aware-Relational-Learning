# Working paper

`Orientation-Aware Relational Learning.pdf` is the August 2026 working paper that motivated the experimental programme. It predates several benchmark results now preserved in this repository.

Treat `docs/CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE.md` as the current claim boundary. In particular, the paper's proposed stability-aware acquisition term was subsequently falsified by v0.2, while the later exact-quotienting and structural-risk results are not reflected in the original paper.
